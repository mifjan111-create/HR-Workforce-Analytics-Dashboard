# HR-Workforce-Analytics-Dashboard
Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, and department performance.

**Business Problem**

HR leaders require workforce insights to monitor employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, and departmental performance.

**Tools Used**

•	Power BI

•	Excel

•	DAX

**Dashboard KPIs**

•	Total Employees

•	Attrition Count

•	Attrition Rate

•	Average Age

•	Average Experience

•	Active Workforce Distribution

**Dashboard Features**

•	Attrition Analysis by Department

•	Attrition Analysis by Salary Slab

•	Employee Demographics

•	Age Group Distribution

•	Gender Distribution

•	Department Workforce Analysis

•	Salary Insights

**Key Insights**

•	Attrition was concentrated in selected departments.

•	Employees aged 26–35 represented the largest workforce segment.

•	Salary levels influenced attrition trends.

•	Workforce distribution varied significantly across departments.

•	Departmental headcount trends highlighted workforce concentration areas.
